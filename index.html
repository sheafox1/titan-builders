How It Works

How a remodel works

One design-build team, four steps.

1. Walk and listen

We walk your space, listen to how you live, and talk through what is realistic.

2. Design and selections

Layout, cabinets, counters and finishes chosen with our designer before anything is ordered.

3. Permits and schedule

We pull the permits and give you a schedule built around your scope and lead times.

4. Build and walkthrough

Our crews build it, keep you updated, and walk every detail with you at the end.
